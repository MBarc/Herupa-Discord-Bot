Chat bridge

In-game chat, joins, deaths, and advancements post into this channel, and messages typed there are relayed in game. Moving it moves both directions (the feed's webhook follows). "(off)" stops the Discord-to-game relay; the in-game feed keeps posting until the minecraft feature is turned off. Also works in Discord: $mcbridge here / #channel / off.

Role sync

Members with a linked Minecraft account get the LuckPerms group of each Discord role they hold. Clear a row's group (or set its role to none) to drop the mapping. What a group can do in game is configured in LuckPerms itself.
